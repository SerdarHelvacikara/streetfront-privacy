Streetfront for Android — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · This page covers the Android version from Google Play. The iPhone and iPad version has its own policy: index.html.

This policy explains what information the Android version of the game Streetfront (the "App") uses and why. The App is made by Serdar Helvacıkara ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to helvacikaraserdar@gmail.com.

1. What we collect ourselves

Nothing. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. Your game progress, coins and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not use analytics.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a full-screen ad now and then between rooms or after a run (at most about one every three minutes, never during a fight), and optional video ads you can choose to watch to continue a run or to double the coins of a run.

To show and measure these ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK in the App collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AD_ID);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version);
  • ad interactions (for example which ads were shown or tapped);
  • diagnostic information (for example crash and performance data of the ads SDK).

Google uses this data to deliver ads, to measure them, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to personalise ads. How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Your choices

  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the App asks for your consent (Google's consent form) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App: Settings > PRIVACY OPTIONS.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teens (Google's "T" rating).

4. Purchases

The App offers optional coin packs as in-app purchases, processed entirely by Google Play Billing: we never see or store your payment details or your Google account. The App only records on your device which purchases have already been delivered, so the coins are never given twice. Everything in the App can also be unlocked with coins earned by playing.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data retention and deletion

Game data is stored only on your device: you can delete it at any time by deleting the App. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted according to Google's policies; you can reset your advertising ID as described above.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page before the changed version of the App is released.
