Streetfront — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Streetfront (the "App") uses and why. The App is made by Serdar Helvacıkara ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to helvacikaraserdar@gmail.com.

1. Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, advertising identifier or any other personal information. Your game progress and settings (for example sound, music and vibration settings) are stored only on your device. The App works offline, we do not run our own servers, we do not use analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share any data.

2. Advertising

The current version of the App shows no advertising and contains no advertising or tracking code. If a future version adds optional ads (for example a video you can choose to watch to continue a run), we will update this policy before that version is released, describe exactly which provider is used and what it collects, and ask for your permission where the law, Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules or Google Play's policies require it.

3. Purchases

The App offers optional coin packs as in-app purchases. They are processed entirely by the store you installed the App from (Apple In-App Purchase on iPhone and iPad, Google Play Billing on Android): we never see or store your payment details, your Apple ID or Google account, or any other personal information. The App only records on your device which purchases have already been delivered, so the coins are never given twice. Everything in the App can also be unlocked with coins earned by playing.

4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Data retention and deletion

Because game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it at any time by deleting the App.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page before the changed version of the App is released. Significant changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
