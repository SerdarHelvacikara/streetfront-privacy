Frequently asked questions

Do I need the internet? No. Streetfront plays fully offline and needs no account.

I was knocked down. How do I continue? Tap CALL MEDEVAC on the "You're down" screen to get back into the fight, or RETREAT to end the run.

How do I turn sound, music or vibration off? On the title screen tap SETTINGS (top right).

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game? Progress and settings are stored only on your device, so deleting the game deletes them.

Does the game collect my data? No. See the Privacy Policy.
